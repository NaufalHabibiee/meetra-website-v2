Questions

Often yes, using external sensors or gateways. Feasibility is checked during a site assessment.

Where is IoT data stored?

On-premise, cloud or hybrid options are chosen during design based on connectivity, volume and data policies.

Is training available as a separate service?

Yes. Training programs can be delivered on their own or as part of a technology implementation.
