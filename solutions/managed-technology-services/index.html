Questions

Can managed services cover only part of our IT?

Yes. Scope can be limited to specific systems, locations or service hours and expanded later.

How are service levels defined?

Response and resolution targets, support hours and reporting are agreed in the service level agreement before the service starts.

Do you take over systems built by other vendors?

Yes, after a handover assessment to understand the system, its documentation and any known issues.
